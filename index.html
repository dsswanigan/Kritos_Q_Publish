Methodology

Equity Curve

Current Holdings

Symbol% of PortfolioSharesFirst EnteredAverage Entry PriceCurrent PriceP&L %Max DD %MFE %Win

Holdings are the positions the book currently carries, one row per instrument. % of Portfolio is the position's share of the book; Shares is the number held; Average Entry Price is the close the stock was bought at; P&L % is its total return since then, dividends included; Max DD % and MFE % are the worst drawdown and best gain of the position since first entry.

Latest Actions

Stocks are sold first and their whole proceeds buy the incoming stocks; positions are never rebalanced. A Stocks line means the risk overlay is changing the share of the book held in stocks (the rest is in BIL); it scales every stock position by the same factor.

Closed Trades

SymbolSignal (Fri)EnteredSoldDaysShares% of BookExitReturn %Max DD %MFE %WinP&L $

Every row is one completed position, from entry to exit. Return % is the position's own return after costs; P&L $ is what it added to the book after the risk overlay's scaling.
