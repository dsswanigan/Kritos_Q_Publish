Methodology

Equity Curve

Current Holdings

Symbol% of PortfolioSharesFirst EnteredAverage Entry PriceCurrent PriceP&L %Max DD %MFE %Win

Holdings are the positions the book currently carries, one row per instrument. % of Portfolio is the position's share of the book; Shares is the number held; Average Entry Price is the close the stock was first bought at; P&L % is its total return since then, dividends included; Max DD % and MFE % are the worst and best close since the stock was bought, measured against the purchase price, not against the position's own high: a stock that has never closed below its purchase price shows 0.00% however far it has come off its peak.

Latest Actions

Once a month the book rebalances: stocks leaving the ten are sold, newcomers are bought, and every holding is reset to an equal share of the book (10%). Sells are listed first, then buys, then resizes.

Closed Trades

SymbolSignal (Fri)EnteredSoldDaysShares% of BookExitReturn %Max DD %MFE %WinP&L $

Every row is one completed position, from entry to exit. Return % is the stock's own return from purchase to sale, after costs; P&L $ is what the position added to the book while it was held (its size changes at each monthly reset).
